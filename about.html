About Crime Analytics Portal

This web application generates statistical crime analysis based on historical records from 2021 to 2026.

  • Dataset Integration: Automatically synced from indian_crime_data_2021_2026_with_names.csv into SQLite.
  • Server-Side Chart Rendering: Powered by Matplotlib and Seaborn directly in Flask for zero rendering bugs.
  • Officer Management: Authenticated portal for updating official records.
